How to Play Reverse Taboo

        
      

      

        

          
1

          

            
The Premise

            
In traditional Taboo, a player describes a word without saying a list of forbidden words. In Reverse Taboo, the roles are flipped: you are given the prohibited words, and you must deduce the secret target word!

          

        

        

          
2

          

            
Progressive Clues (Daily Mode)

            
You start each word with only 2 clues revealed. Every wrong guess unlocks another prohibited clue card. You can also press "Reveal Another Clue" at any time for a 5-second timer penalty.

          

        

        

          
3

          

            
Reverse Blitz Mode

            
A fast-paced decreasing difficulty challenge! Round 1 gives you all clues, Round 2 gives you 4 clues, and Round 3 gives you only 2 clues. Race against the clock!

          

        

        

          
4

          

            
Typo Forgiveness

            
Don't worry about minor spelling mistakes! Our intelligent fuzzy engine forgives typos, drops parenthetical suffixes (e.g. "Go (game)" → "Go"), and will nudge you if you are warm!
